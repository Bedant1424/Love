## Design Notes

- **Layout:** A4 Portrait (210mm × 297mm), viewBox 595 × 842pt
- **Page Header:** Poppins Bold 8pt, LeadFlow Blue, top left. "Page 13 / Return on Investment" top right. Thin separator at y=52.
- **Hero:** Poppins Bold 24pt, Slate 900, left-aligned. Subtitle Inter 9.5pt, Slate 500.
- **Body:** Inter Regular 10pt, Slate 900, max ~70 chars per line.
- **Dominant Visual:** Two-Column Workflow Comparison Layout (Height increased by 20% to 220pt).
  - Comparison Card: x=42, y=180, width=511, height=220, rx=12. White background (#FFFFFF), border #E5E7EB, shadow.
    - Left Column (Manual Process): Red heading, vertical list of manual tracking limitations with red monochrome outline icons (Spreadsheet, WhatsApp, Warning).
    - Right Column (LeadFlow Workflow): Blue heading, vertical list of automated workflows with blue monochrome outline icons (Database, Dashboard, Check Circle).
    - Center Transition: Subtle blue arrow at cx=297.5, cy=290 connecting the processes (strengthened with larger circle r=18, stroke-width 1.5, and larger arrow).
  - Outcome Cards (5): Configured in a two-row grid below the comparison card.
    - Row 1 (y=416, height=60, rx=8): Three cards representing Reduced Manual Work (x=42), Faster Assignment (x=216), and Better Accountability (x=391).
    - Row 2 (y=488, height=60, rx=8): Two cards representing Real-Time Visibility (x=129) and Predictable Operations (x=303), centered under the top row.
    - Each card contains a green check-circle icon, Poppins SemiBold 8.5pt title, and Inter Regular 7pt description (with keywords bolded).
- **Takeaway:** Reusable master Takeaway component, y=640, height=64, Inter Medium 11pt, Slate 900.
- **Footer:** Inter Regular 7pt, Slate 400. Page number right-aligned at y=822.
- **Spacing:** 8-point system throughout.
