## Page Content

### Page Header

LeadFlow brand reference (top left, consistent across all pages).
Page 03 identifier with section label (top right).

### Hero Section

**Current Challenges**

Understanding the recruitment workflow that needs to change.

### Introduction

Recruitment teams managing leads through manual tools
face predictable challenges that reduce speed, visibility,
and accountability across daily operations.

### Challenge Grid (Dominant Visual — 3×3)

| # | Challenge | Icon | Description |
|---|-----------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Manual Excel Tracking | FileSpreadsheet | Leads tracked in spreadsheets with no real-time sync or versioning. |
| 2 | WhatsApp Reporting | MessageCircle | Updates shared through chat with no structured format or archive. |
| 3 | Duplicate Leads | Copy | Same leads contacted by multiple team members. |
| 4 | Poor Lead Ownership | UserX | No clear assignment record. Leads left untracked. |
| 5 | No Central Dashboard | LayoutDashboard | No single view of activity, progress, or pipeline status. |
| 6 | No Performance Tracking | EyeOff | No way to compare output, call volume, or conversions. |
| 7 | No Historical Reports | Clock | Past data scattered across files with no searchable history. |
| 8 | Manual Distribution | Users | Leads assigned through chat, creating delays and imbalance. |
| 9 | Limited Accountability | AlertTriangle | No audit trail connecting actions to individuals. |

### Business Impact

These operational gaps cost time every day.
Without structured tools, teams work harder but achieve less.

### Key Takeaway

> These challenges are not unique — they are the direct result of managing recruitment without a centralized platform.
