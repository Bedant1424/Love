**Introducing LeadFlow**

One platform to centralize your recruitment operations.

### Introduction

LeadFlow is a cloud-based Recruitment Operations Platform. It centralizes lead management, assignments, and reporting into one workspace.

Interns access their assigned leads on any device, while managers monitor progress from a live dashboard.

Accessible from any modern browser with a fast, app-like experience. No installation required.

### Key Takeaway
