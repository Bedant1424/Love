## Page Content

### Page Header

LeadFlow brand reference (top left, consistent across all pages).
Page 09 identifier with section label (top right).

### Hero Section

**Analytics Dashboard**

Actionable business intelligence and automated performance reporting.

### Introduction

LeadFlow automatically converts recruitment activity into meaningful business insights. Managers no longer need to prepare reports manually or consolidate spreadsheets before reviewing performance.

The system gathers granular metrics from every call, message, and lead transition to generate live updates.
