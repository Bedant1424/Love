**Lead Management**

### Introduction

LeadFlow replaces scattered spreadsheets with a secure, centralized lead database. Managers import lead batches, filter database records, and allocate assignments to interns from a unified interface.

With built-in duplicate detection and automated assignment rules, every lead is accounted for and assigned to a clear owner.
