## 1. Dynamic QR Competitor Architecture

All major commercial dynamic QR platforms (Uniqode, Bitly, Flowcode, QR Code Generator) operate on the identical 3-step paradigm:
$$\text{Printed QR (Permanent Slug)} \longrightarrow \text{Edge Lookup} \longrightarrow \text{HTTP 301/302 Redirect to Target}$$

### Competitor Pricing vs QRoute
- **Uniqode:** \$15–\$99/month.
- **Bitly:** \$10–\$199/month (Free plan limits dynamic QR to 2 codes).
- **Flowcode:** \$300–\$3,000/year.
- **The SaaS Trap:** Canceling the subscription immediately disables all physical cards.
- **QRoute Advantage:** Permanent \$0/month edge routing that never locks physical cards hostage.

## 2. Google Destination URL Standards & Flexible Validation

Google Business Profile allows merchants to obtain their review link directly from Google Search, Google Maps, or the Business Profile manager. Because merchants share links obtained across different Google entry points, the system must **NOT** hardcode a single brittle URL pattern.

### Approved Google Hostnames & Path Structures:
1. **Canonical Review Dialog (`search.google.com`):**
   - Format: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid={PLACE_ID}`
   - Launches 5-star review rating modal directly.
2. **Google Business Profile Short Link (`g.page`):**
   - Format: `https://g.page/r/{CODE}/review` or `https://g.page/{SLUG}/review`
   - Generated in Business Profile dashboard.
3. **Google Maps Mobile App Share Link (`maps.app.goo.gl`):**
   - Format: `https://maps.app.goo.gl/{TOKEN}`
   - Generated by mobile Google Maps app "Share" feature.
4. **Google Maps Desktop / CID Link (`maps.google.com`, `www.google.com`):**
   - Format: `https://maps.google.com/maps?...` or `https://www.google.com/maps/place/...`

### Centralized Validator Module Invariant:
All hostname and path allowances are encapsulated in `src/shared/google-url-validator.ts`. If Google introduces or updates review link structures in the future, exactly **one** centralized code update is required.
